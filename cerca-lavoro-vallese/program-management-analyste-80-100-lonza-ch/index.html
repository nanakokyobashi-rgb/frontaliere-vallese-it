Descrizione completa

The actual location of this job is in Visp, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

Lonza in Visp is looking for a Program Management Analyst. You will engage with and provide clarity to the Lonza Business Management and Program Management Team by translating the data within the data collection tools into informative consolidated views. These views will become essential to drive data-driven action and will be the framework for your analytical advice and support using language that can be easily understood by non-technical colleagues. You will also ensure all business critical reporting is up-to-date and available in a timely manner with a role of data integrity ambassador for the site, acting as a point of contact for first stage resolution of data integrity issues.

The successful candidate will use their strong business management and financial tool background together with their analysis expertise in transforming data into information by constructing new and tailoring old tools in our business and as such will have a high level of direct exposure to our Senior Leadership Team.
